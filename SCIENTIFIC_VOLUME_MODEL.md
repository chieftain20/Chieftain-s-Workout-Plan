# Chieftain Scientific Weekly Muscle Volume Model

## Counting rules

- **Direct contribution:** 1.0 set.
- **Meaningful indirect contribution:** 0.5 set.
- **Stability / corrective / activation drills:** tracked separately and not added to hypertrophy effective-set totals.

The 0.5 fractional-set convention is used as a practical model for indirect work. It is an estimate of training volume, not a direct measurement of muscle stimulus.

## Formula

`Effective sets = direct sets + (indirect sets × 0.5)`

Example:

- 3 Hack Squat sets → Quadriceps: 3.0 effective sets.
- The same 3 Hack Squat sets → Glutes: 1.5 effective sets.

## Important interpretation

Effective sets are an accounting tool for comparing weekly training exposure. They should not be interpreted as an exact physiological measurement of hypertrophic stimulus. Exercise technique, range of motion, proximity to failure, loading, individual anatomy and exercise selection all affect the actual stimulus.

RIR is therefore kept as a separate training-quality variable and is **not** converted into an arbitrary volume multiplier.
